# 003 Within SLE daily record stores the SLE in force
Status: Proposed

## Context

```text
Story 6563 shows how the share of closed Work Items within the SLE develops day by day. The SLE exists only as two current values on a Team or Portfolio, so any series computed on read would re-measure past days against today SLE.
```

## Decision

```text
Persist a WithinSleSnapshot per owner, horizon (30/60/90) and day with the closed and within counts, the SLE percentile and days in force, and whether the day was recorded or filled in. One writer records today on refresh and fills absent past days from stored history; it never rewrites a past row. The share and the minimum of 5 are decided by the WithinSleShare value object for both the widget and the chart.
```

## Alternatives

```text
Compute the series on read against today SLE (rejected: rewrites history when the SLE changes, Q4). Store percentages (rejected: loses the count and rounds). Extend percentiles-over-time (rejected: different row shape and external client consumers). Keep the share in the frontend (rejected: the record must be computed in the backend and two owners of one rule drift).
```

## Consequences

```text
A fourth day-keyed snapshot table and its migration; the filler fills one more family per pass; filled-in days before recording use the SLE set at fill time and the chart says so; Cycle Time Percentiles keeps its own frontend count until a follow-up moves it.
```
