without alteration because there is no grant to publish a modified version of
that text. Keeping it intact and adding our own terms in a second document is
the honest way to build on it.

**Why the software is still public.** Everything above restricts what you may
provide to other people. None of it restricts your ability to read the source,
audit it, run it, or change it for yourself. That is deliberate: the ability to
verify that Lighthouse keeps your delivery data on your own infrastructure is
the point of publishing the source, and it survives these terms intact.
